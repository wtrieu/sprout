The parts sheet

This is the whole asset set behind the lab: hand-placed grids. It is also exactly what you would ask Midjourney for, once per style, before slicing it into layers.

What to judge

  • Combinatorial feel. Six slots with 3–4 options each already give characters. Every new part multiplies, which is the Dungeon Crawler Carl property.
  • Reward legibility. A cape, a held item and an aura each read at a glance. That is what a loot box has to deliver in one frame.
  • Animation cost. The idle bob and sparkle twinkle here are two frames per layer. Every new animation is a per-layer sprite sheet, never a re-draw of the whole character. (Motion is off if your system asks for reduced motion.)
  • What it can't do. Parts must share one grid and one palette, so every style is a separate full parts sheet. A Midjourney-only route makes nicer single images and no combinations.
